## 1. Getting Started

### What is Pastro?

Pastro is a mobile application that allows you to browse products, place orders, and track your purchases in real-time. The app provides a seamless shopping experience with live order status updates and easy order management.

### System Requirements

**Minimum Requirements:**
- Android 8.0+ or iOS 13.0+
- 200 MB free storage space
- Internet connection (WiFi or mobile data)
- 2 GB RAM

**Recommended:**
- Android 12+ or iOS 15+
- 4 GB+ RAM
- Stable WiFi or 4G/5G connection

### Key Features

✅ **User Authentication** - Secure login and registration  
✅ **Product Browsing** - Browse extensive product catalog  
✅ **Order Management** - Create, view, and track orders  
✅ **Real-time Updates** - Live order status notifications  
✅ **Profile Management** - Update your profile information  
✅ **Stock Tracking** - Check product availability

## 2. Installation

### Installing on Android

1. **Download the App**
   - Open Google Play Store
   - Search for "Pastro"
   - Tap "Install"

2. **Allow Permissions**
   - Camera: For photo uploads (optional)
   - Photos/Media: For selecting images
   - Contacts: For customer information (optional)

3. **Launch the App**
   - Tap "Open" after installation completes
   - Or find the Pastro icon on your home screen

### Installing on iOS

1. **Download the App**
   - Open Apple App Store
   - Search for "Pastro"
   - Tap the cloud icon to download

2. **Allow Permissions**
   - Camera: For photo uploads (optional)
   - Photos: For selecting images
   - Contacts: For customer information (optional)

3. **Launch the App**
   - Tap "Open" after installation completes
   - Or find the Pastro icon on your home screen

### Troubleshooting Installation

**Issue:** "Not enough storage space"
- Solution: Delete unused apps or files and try again

**Issue:** "App won't install"
- Solution: Restart your phone and try again. Or uninstall any previous version.

**Issue:** "Permission denied"
- Solution: Go to Settings > Apps > Pastro > Permissions and enable required permissions

## 3. User Account Management

### Creating an Account (Registration)

1. **Launch the App**
   - Open Pastro on your phone

2. **Tap "Register"**
   - If you're on the login screen, select the "Register" option
   - Or tap "Don't have an account? Register" at the bottom

3. **Fill in Your Details**
   - **Full Name:** Enter your first and last name
   - **Email:** Enter a valid email address
   - **Password:** Create a strong password (at least 8 characters)
   - **Confirm Password:** Re-enter your password

4. **Review Terms**
   - Read and accept Terms of Service
   - Check "I agree to the Terms"

5. **Submit Registration**
   - Tap "REGISTER" button
   - You'll see: "Registration successful! A verification email has been sent..."

6. **Verify Your Email**
   - Check your email inbox for verification link
   - Click the verification link to confirm your account
   - Return to the app to log in

### Logging In

1. **Enter Credentials**
   - **Email:** Enter your registered email
   - **Password:** Enter your password

2. **Tap "LOGIN"**
   - If credentials are correct, you'll proceed to the home screen
   - If incorrect, you'll see an error message

3. **Stay Logged In**
   - Select "Remember me" to stay logged in
   - Your session persists across app restarts

### Logging Out

1. **Go to Profile Tab**
   - Tap the Profile icon (👤) at the bottom

2. **Tap "Logout"**
   - Confirm logout when prompted
   - You'll return to the login screen

### Password Recovery

1. **On Login Screen**
   - Tap "Forgot Password?"

2. **Enter Email**
   - Enter the email associated with your account

3. **Check Email**
   - You'll receive a password reset link
   - Click the link to reset your password

## 6. Creating Orders

### Step 1: Select Product

1. **Browse Products Tab**
2. **Tap on Product** you want to order
3. **Review Product Details**

### Step 2: Configure Order

1. **Tap "Create Order"** button
2. **Enter Customer Name**
   - Required field
   - Enter your full name or business name

3. **Set Quantity**
   - Enter number of items (minimum: 1)
   - See unit price and total calculation

4. **Select Material** (Optional)
   - Tap to choose material option
   - Defaults to product material if not selected

5. **Select Color** (Optional)
   - Tap to choose color option
   - Defaults to product color if not selected

### Step 3: Review Order

- **Review Summary**
  - Product name and details
  - Quantity selected
  - Unit price (₱)
  - Total price (₱)
  - Material and color selection

- **Make Changes**
  - Tap fields to modify
  - Update quantity, material, or color

### Step 4: Submit Order

1. **Tap "PLACE ORDER"** button
2. **Confirmation**
   - "Your order for [Product] has been placed successfully"
   - Order ID provided
   - Estimated delivery time shown

3. **Next Steps**
   - Tap "OK" to close confirmation
   - Go to Orders tab to track status

### Order Creation Failed?

**Possible Issues & Solutions:**

| Error | Cause | Solution |
|-------|-------|----------|
| "customer_name is required" | Forgot to enter name | Enter your name in the field |
| "Invalid quantity" | Qty < 1 or not numeric | Enter whole number ≥ 1 |
| "Product out of stock" | Item not available | Choose different product |
| "Network error" | No internet connection | Check WiFi/mobile data |
| "Order creation failed" | Server error | Try again in a few minutes |

## 7. Managing Orders

### Viewing Orders List

1. **Tap Orders Tab**
   - See all your orders displayed
   - Most recent orders shown first

2. **Order Information Displayed**
   - Order ID
   - Product name
   - Order date
   - Current status
   - Total amount

### Order Statuses

| Status | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| 🟡 **Pending** | Order received, awaiting processing |
| 🔵 **Processing** | Staff is preparing your order |
| 📦 **Ready** | Order ready for delivery/pickup |
| ✅ **Completed** | Order delivered/picked up |
| ❌ **Cancelled** | Order was cancelled |

### Real-time Order Updates

1. **Live Notifications**
   - Push notifications when status changes
   - Automatic app update without refresh

2. **Enable Notifications**
   - Go to Settings tab
   - Toggle "Order Notifications" ON
   - Ensure app permissions allow notifications

### Viewing Order Details

1. **Tap Order** from Orders list
2. **See Full Details**
   - Product information
   - Quantity and price breakdown
   - Current status with timestamp
   - Estimated completion date
   - Material and color specifications

3. **Contact Support**
   - If issues with order, tap "Contact Support"
   - Opens email client with pre-filled info

### Cancelling an Order

1. **View Order Details**
2. **Tap "Cancel Order"** (if status allows)
   - Available for: Pending, Processing
   - Not available for: Ready, Completed, Cancelled

3. **Confirm Cancellation**
   - Review warning message
   - Tap "Confirm" to cancel
   - Refund will be processed

## 8. Profile Management

### Accessing Profile

1. **Tap Profile Tab** (👤)

   - Full name

### Updating Profile

1. **From Profile Screen**
   - Tap "Edit Profile"

   - **Phone:** Add or update phone number
   - **Address:** Add delivery address

3. **Save Changes**

   - Changes applied immediately

### Changing Password

1. **From Profile Screen**
   - Tap "Change Password"

2. **Enter Passwords**
   - **Current Password:** Enter existing password
   - **New Password:** Enter new password
   - **Confirm Password:** Re-enter new password

3. **Validation**
   - Password must be 8+ characters
   - Include uppercase, lowercase, numbers
   - Must be different from current password

4. **Save**
   - Tap "Update Password"
   - You'll need to log in again with new password

- **Enable Two-Factor Authentication** (recommended)
  - Settings > Security > Enable 2FA
  - Adds extra protection to your account

- **View Login History**

  - See where and when account is accessed

## 9. Troubleshooting

### Common Issues

#### Issue: App Won't Start
**Solution:**
1. Force close the app
2. Restart your phone
3. Clear app cache: Settings > Apps > Pastro > Storage > Clear Cache
4. Reinstall app if problem persists

#### Issue: Login Not Working
**Solution:**
1. Verify internet connection
2. Check email and password are correct
3. Ensure email is verified
4. Try resetting password

#### Issue: Orders Not Showing
**Solution:**
1. Pull down to refresh orders list
2. Check internet connection
3. Log out and log back in
4. Ensure you have orders in your account

#### Issue: Real-time Updates Not Working
**Solution:**
1. Check internet connection (WiFi/4G)
2. Go to Settings > Notifications > Enable
3. Restart the app
4. Check notification permissions in phone settings

#### Issue: Order Creation Failed
**Solution:**
1. Verify all required fields are filled
2. Check product is in stock
3. Ensure quantity is valid (≥ 1)
4. Try again after few seconds

#### Issue: Payment/Billing Issues
**Solution:**
1. Verify payment method is active
2. Check account has sufficient balance
3. Contact support with order details
4. Email: support@pastro.app

### Performance Issues

**App Running Slowly:**
1. Close background apps
2. Clear app cache
3. Free up phone storage (>1GB)
4. Restart phone
5. Update app to latest version

**Battery Drain:**
1. Disable location services
2. Reduce screen brightness
3. Close notifications
4. Limit background app refresh

## 10. FAQ

### General Questions

**Q: Is my data secure?**
A: Yes, we use encryption for all data transmission and secure password hashing. Your information is protected by industry-standard security protocols.

**Q: How often is my order updated?**
A: Orders are updated in real-time through our notification system. Enable notifications in settings to receive instant updates.

**Q: Can I use the app offline?**
A: No, internet connection is required for all features. However, previously loaded content may be viewable temporarily.

### Account Questions

**Q: Can I have multiple accounts?**
A: No, each email can only have one account. You can register with a different email to create a new account.

**Q: How do I delete my account?**
A: Go to Settings > Account > Delete Account. Your data will be permanently removed.

**Q: What if I forget my password?**
A: Tap "Forgot Password?" on the login screen to reset it via email.

**Q: Can I change my email address?**
A: Yes, go to Profile > Edit Profile > Email. You'll need to verify the new email.

### Order Questions

**Q: How long does delivery take?**
A: Delivery time depends on your location and current order volume. Check the order details for estimated delivery date.

**Q: Can I cancel an order?**
A: Orders can be cancelled if status is "Pending" or "Processing". Once marked "Ready", cancellation may not be possible.

**Q: Can I modify an order after creation?**
A: No, once created, orders cannot be modified. You must cancel and create a new order.

**Q: What if my order is wrong?**
A: Contact support immediately with your order number. Include photos if items are damaged.

**Q: Do you offer refunds?**
A: Yes, refunds are available for cancelled orders and defective items. Review our return policy.

### Technical Questions

**Q: What devices are supported?**
A: Android 8.0+ and iOS 13.0+. Check the app store for compatibility with your device.

**Q: Does the app work internationally?**
A: Currently available in the Philippines. International availability depends on shipping capabilities.

**Q: How much data does the app use?**
A: Approximately 5-10 MB per month for regular use. Heavy usage may increase data consumption.

**Q: Can I use the app on tablet?**
A: Yes, the app is optimized for phones but works on tablets running supported OS versions.

### Billing Questions

**Q: How do you calculate shipping?**
A: Shipping is calculated based on location and order weight. See checkout for exact amount.

**Q: What payment methods are accepted?**
A: Check the payment screen for accepted methods (cards, e-wallets, bank transfer).

**Q: When will I be charged?**
A: You are charged when you place the order (upon payment confirmation).
